RollCD Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

RollCD ("the app") is made by the team behind Roll Booth (rollbooth.com). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Your photos and videos stay on your device

The app uses your camera (and your microphone, when you record video) to take photos and videos. It saves them inside the app and, when you tap Save, to your photo library. The app reads your photo library only for photos you pick to edit, and to save into a "RollCD" album.

We do not upload, collect or see your photos, videos or recordings. They leave your device only when you share them yourself, through your phone's share sheet.

There are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads only when you choose to watch one, to unlock a premium disc or HD export. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android),
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address,
  • information about ad interactions,
  • diagnostics, such as crash and performance data from the ad software.

This is used to show, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. On iOS, the app asks your permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before using the advertising identifier. If you decline, you still get ads, just not personalised ones. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice from the app's Settings.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes, it may send a crash report to Sentry: device model, OS
version, app version and technical details of the error. Crash reports contain
no photos and no personal information, and are used only to fix bugs.
Sentry's privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

Stored on your device

The app keeps your settings and unlock status on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, along with any photos you hadn't saved to your library.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.
