RollCD Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026 · Last updated: September 30, 2026

RollCD ("the app") is published by i0 labs (developer: Sarath Ramesh), Thanjavur, Tamil Nadu, India. Contact: hello@rollbooth.com.

This policy explains what the app does with your information. It covers RollCD on iOS (bundle ID com.rollcd.app) and on Android (package com.polaroidbooth.app).

Your photos and videos stay on your device

The app uses your camera (and your microphone, when you record video) to take photos and videos. They are processed and stored only on your device: inside the app and, when you tap Save, in your photo library. The app reads your photo library only for photos you pick to edit, and to save into a "RollCD" album.

We do not upload, collect or see your photos, videos or recordings. They leave your device only when you share them yourself, through your phone's share sheet.

There are no accounts, and we don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads only when you choose to watch one, to unlock a premium disc or HD export. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may collect:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android),
  • your approximate location, derived from your IP address,
  • information about ad interactions,
  • diagnostics, such as crash and performance data from the ad software.

This is used to show, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud. On iOS, the app asks your permission to track (App Tracking Transparency) before using the advertising identifier. If you decline, you still get ads, just not personalised ones. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form before any ad is requested.

Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
How Google uses data from partner apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes or hits an error, it may send a report to Sentry: device model, OS version, app version and technical details of the error. The app sends errors only. It does not send screenshots, photos, a user ID or performance traces, and it is set not to send personal information such as your IP address. Reports are used only to fix bugs. Sentry processes this data in the United States or the European Union, depending on the project's region.

Sentry's privacy policy: https://sentry.io/privacy/

Your choices

  • iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, then turn off tracking for RollCD.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads, then delete or reset your advertising ID.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: in the app, open Settings > "Ad privacy choices" to reopen Google's consent form and change your answer.

Opting out does not turn ads off. It makes them non-personalised.

Data retention and deletion

We store no personal data on servers of our own. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days and then deleted. For advertising data held by Google, see Google's privacy policy above. You can email hello@rollbooth.com to ask us to delete any crash reports tied to your device.

The app keeps your settings and unlock status on your device. Deleting the app deletes them, along with any photos you hadn't saved to your library.

Legal bases (EEA and UK)

  • Consent for personalised advertising.
  • Legitimate interest for crash reporting and for preventing ad fraud.

Sale or sharing of personal information

We do not sell personal information. Advertising data shared with Google AdMob may count as "sharing" under California law. You can opt out with the controls in "Your choices" above.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the dates at the top.
